## 📋 **IMAGE GENERATION SPECIFICATIONS**

### **Brand Guidelines for All Images**
- ✅ Use navy (#0A0E27) and gold (#f4b942) as primary colors
- ✅ Use modern, clean design style
- ✅ Include official Bangladeshi context (payment methods, delivery time)
- ✅ Optimize for mobile (responsive, clear on small screens)
- ✅ Include WhatsApp icon/color (#25d366) where relevant
- ✅ Professional, trustworthy aesthetic
- ✅ Clear CTAs or next steps
- ✅ Readable text (minimum 14px for body)

### **Image Dimensions** (SEO-optimized)
- **Hero banners**: 1200x600px (16:9)
- **Social cards**: 1080x1080px (1:1)
- **Product thumbnails**: 300x300px (1:1)
- **Infographics**: 800x800px or 1000x600px
- **Email headers**: 600x200px (3:1)

### **Delivery Format**
- All images: WebP format (fast loading, 50% smaller than PNG)
- Alt formats: JPG fallback for older browsers
- Compressed but high quality

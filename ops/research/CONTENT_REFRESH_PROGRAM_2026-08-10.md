# AIPS Continuous Product Truth & Content Refresh Program

Checked: 2026-08-10 (BDT)

## Objective
Keep every AIPS public page useful, current, customer-first and source-traceable while preserving a strict firewall between provider facts and AIPS commercial approval.

## Non-negotiable rules
1. Official provider sources are the primary authority for product plans, features, limits, terms and availability.
2. A provider fact never auto-approves an AIPS SKU, resale method, price, access model, payment channel, delivery SLA, warranty/refund or margin.
3. Customer-facing copy must answer a real problem, buying decision or workflow question. Thin keyword pages and doorway pages are rejected.
4. Stale provider facts must be refreshed or marked unknown; they must not remain evergreen by assumption.
5. Historical catalog data remains history. Public projection must use current evidence layers.
6. Shared-password, pooled-login or recovery-control arrangements remain quarantined unless the exact provider-authorized model is documented and separately approved.
7. No ranking, customer-count, revenue, rating, partnership, speed, warranty or performance superlative is published without current evidence.

## Required product evidence fields
Every product/product-family refresh should track:
- provider
- product/tier
- official source URLs
- source checked date
- provider price and regional variability
- current features/capabilities
- usage/credit/seat model
- account/workspace ownership model
- provider policy/compliance notes
- customer problem / job-to-be-done
- solution summary
- best-fit users
- limitations / who should not buy
- alternatives/comparison intent
- Bangladesh-specific payment/access friction where factually supported
- AIPS commercial approval status (separate SSOT)
- next refresh date

## Page-quality checklist
For each product, category, comparison, role and blog page:
- One clear primary search intent.
- One clear customer problem.
- First screen explains who the page is for and what decision it helps make.
- Current provider facts are sourced and dated.
- Copy explains limitations and tradeoffs, not only benefits.
- Product-specific claims are not copied across unrelated pages.
- Internal links connect product -> comparison -> role/JTBD -> tutorial -> support/trust.
- Title, H1 and description match the actual content.
- Canonical/hreflang/schema are correct and non-duplicative.
- Mobile, keyboard, reduced-motion and contrast checks pass.
- Commercial CTAs fail closed when offer evidence is not approved.
- Content has a refresh owner/state and evidence timestamp.

## Priority refresh order
### P0 truth/compliance
- ChatGPT shared-access variants and any provider-policy conflicts.
- Fixed provider pricing that is actually regional/variable.
- Hard-coded delivery/warranty/customer-count/product-count claims.
- Old model names/features that providers have superseded.
- Any product with missing official source or checked date.

### P1 revenue-supporting customer intent
- ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini / Perplexity decision guides.
- Midjourney / Ideogram / Runway / HeyGen creator workflows.
- ElevenLabs / Suno audio and commercial-rights explainers.
- GitHub Copilot / Cursor / Replit / v0 coding-workflow comparisons.
- Notion / Gamma productivity and team-workflow pages.
- CapCut current plan/price-variability explainer.

### P2 scalable topical authority
- Student, freelancer, creator, developer, marketer, researcher and SME playbooks.
- Bangladesh payment/security/account-ownership education.
- Practical AI workflows, prompt/process examples, and evaluation checklists.
- Tool-switching and tool-stack guides based on customer jobs rather than keyword permutations.

## Content library target
A 300+ useful-page library is acceptable as a long-horizon outcome only if every page earns its existence through distinct user value. Page count is not a KPI by itself. Consolidate or delete weak/duplicate pages instead of preserving them for SEO volume.

## Measurement loop after release
Use Search Console/analytics/customer-support/CRM evidence to:
1. identify pages with impressions but weak CTR;
2. identify pages with traffic but weak next-step conversion;
3. find recurring customer questions worth new content;
4. refresh facts when provider products change;
5. consolidate cannibalizing pages;
6. improve internal linking around proven topics;
7. track qualified organic enquiries rather than raw traffic alone.
